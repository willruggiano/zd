# Require Wayland protocols rather than a particular compositor

Require layer-shell for the noninteractive indicator and ext-data-control for background clipboard publication, reporting unsupported capabilities clearly instead of adding desktop-specific integrations. The user's initial Hyprland setup is not a product dependency: show the indicator on all monitors without querying compositor-specific focus state, and bind the systemd user service to graphical-session.target with a PipeWire dependency. This keeps the native implementation small and reusable on supporting Wayland compositors at the deliberate cost of not supporting stock GNOME/Mutter or other compositors lacking the required protocols.
